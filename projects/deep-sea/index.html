Chapter OneIT STARTED WITH A FOG.

Zhou Ming was an ordinary middle-school teacher. His life was quiet, predictable, and confined within the walls of his apartment. Then came the day the fog blocked everything.

It was not a weather event. It was a boundary. When he stepped through a mysterious door, the familiar world dissolved, and he found himself connected to an identity that did not belong to him: Duncan Abnomar, the legendary captain of a ghost ship.

"Who knows how to steer this ship?!"

The Ghost ShipA SHIP THAT SHOULD NOT EXIST.

The Vanished is more than a vessel. It is a legend, a curse, and a refuge. It sails through waters where the old order has vanished, where isolated island city-states and brave fleets challenge the endless, supernatural seas.

Beneath the deep sea, shadows from an older world are stirring, waiting to consume what remains. For the new captain, the ship is the starting point of a journey through a world full of strange people, supernatural events, and buried secrets.

He is the captain now. The problem is, he has no idea how to sail the ship.

The SettingTHE SEA HAS ITS OWN SECRETS.

Island City-States

The last lights of civilization. Isolated, fortified, and clinging to existence amidst the boundless ocean.

The Spirit Realm

A dimension intertwined with reality. A place where the rules of the living do not apply and ancient forces linger.

Ancient Forces

Long before the fog, there were other gods and other civilizations. Their remnants are waiting in the deep.

Distorted History

Memories can be altered. History can be rewritten. The past is not a reliable map for the future.

The Endless Fleets

Those who refuse to be confined to islands. They challenge the ocean, seeking resources, knowledge, or salvation.

Mysteries Beneath

The ocean is not simply a setting. It is the central mystery. Every voyage could reveal something extraordinary.

IS THIS YOUR KIND OF STORY?

Deep Sea Embers combines supernatural mysteries, cosmic horror, nautical adventures, and expansive worldbuilding. It balances dark themes with a relatively light and humorous narrative style, making it a unique journey for readers who enjoy long-form Chinese web novels.
