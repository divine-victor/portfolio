A new school, a new friend, and a new way to understand herself

Middle school is a time riddled with change and confusion. Kiera is a bright, quirky 12-year-old who has never quite fit in. She has a happy home life with her two loving moms, but struggles to understand who she is and why she feels so different from her peers.

After starting at a new school, a chance encounter with another "misfit" sets Kiera and her family on a path toward discovering that she is autistic.

The story follows Kiera through seventh grade as she receives her autism diagnosis and begins grappling with what that means for her, both the challenges and the positive discoveries, while also navigating friendships, adolescence, body changes and the everyday difficulties of middle school.

The book is designed to be emotionally evocative while remaining relatable to middle-grade and young-adult readers from diverse backgrounds.

Meet Amber Sayer

Amber Sayer is a professional health and fitness writer from Westfield, Massachusetts. Finally Found is her debut fictional work and represents both a personal passion project and an attempt to fill a void in available literature she experienced when she was Kiera's age.

As an autistic woman, Amber finds she is better able to communicate her thoughts and feelings through writing, and hopes to continue exploring her creativity in future projects.
