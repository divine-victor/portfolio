Web developer & digital marketer

Landing pages for people who have one thing to sell.

I write, design and build each page myself, so the words and the layout are made together.

Someone lands on your page. They don't know you, and they're in no hurry to find out.

So the page answers three questions, in order. What is this? Is it for me? What do I do next?

Colour, motion and photos are there to get a visitor through those three answers, not to compete with them.

And it has to hold up on a phone, because that is often the only screen they'll use.

How it goes

Four steps, no surprises.

You send the basics

What you sell, who buys it, and any copy or links you already have.

I send an outline

Headline and sections in plain text. You approve it before I design anything.

I build and check it

I test every screen size myself, then you review it and I fix what's off.

It goes live

You get the files, or I help you put it on your own domain and hosting.
